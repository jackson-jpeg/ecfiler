# ECFiler Risk Register

**Standing rule:** a risk is logged here exactly once, with its decision.
Once a row exists, the risk is not re-raised in conversation. New
information about a logged risk updates the row; it does not reopen the
discussion. Status values: **ACCEPTED** (known, deliberately living with
it), **MITIGATED** (control in place), **QUEUED** (action owned and
pending).
