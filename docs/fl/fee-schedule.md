## Application fee

- **$500.00 non-refundable application fee**, submitted with the Third Party Vendor Application, mailed to Florida Courts E-Filing Authority, P.O. Box 16428, Tallahassee, FL 32317. (Application, rev. 01-01-2021; also confirmed in Batch Filer FAQs.)

## Monthly license fee (per the "Vendor Fees" PDF, titled "Florida Courts E-Filing Authority Third Party Batch Filing Charges", dated 02/27/2017)

Table heading: "Filings* by Organization"

| Volume tier | Monthly Fee | Annual Fee (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Filings between 1 and 500 | $125 | $1,200 |
| Filings between 501 and 1,000 | $150 | $1,800 |
| Filings between 1,001 and 10,000 | $175 | $2,100 |
| Filings between 10,001 and 25,000 | $200 | $2,400 |
| Filings over 25,000 | $250 | $3,000 |
| Total | $900 | $10,500 |

Footnotes (verbatim from the image):

> \* Filings = documents filed
> Fees will be invoiced monthly based on number of documents filed during the month.
> The Annual fee is not an additional fee; it is shown to demonstrate a possible annualized amount if the same charge was invoiced each month.

## Monthly license fee (binding version, License Agreement Section 3)

Identical tiers ($125 / $150 / $175 / $200 / $250 by documents processed per month). Additional terms:

- Authority may revise the fee structure on 30 days' notice.
- Invoice payable within 30 days; after 30 days, interest and a $25 late charge may apply.
- Unpaid 60 days: Authority may revoke certification and terminate on 10 days' written notice, and refer to collections.
- Failure to pay after final written notice is a stated ground for revocation of certification.

## What is NOT charged

- **No per-filing transaction fee and no revenue share** appears anywhere in the program documents. The only vendor-side charges are the $500 application fee and the tiered monthly license fee.
- **No stated renewal fee.** Certification continues under the license agreement; re-application (and presumably a new $500 fee) is required only after revocation/termination, or to add a new filing path/division (per the Request for Certification form: "To obtain certification in an additional filing path or division, a new Application would be required").
- Statutory court filing fees for the underlying cases still apply and are settled through the Portal at submission time (see technical-specs.md). Portal payment convenience fees (per the Authority FAQ page, https://www.myflcourtaccess.com/authority/faqs): credit cards 3.5% of the filing fee; electronic check $5 flat; payment method settled at the time the filing is submitted.
