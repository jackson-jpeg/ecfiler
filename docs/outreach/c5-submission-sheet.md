# C5 — Clerk inquiry: how to actually send it

**The Middle District of Florida does not publish an email address for CM/ECF
questions.** It publishes a web form, and that is the channel:

**<https://www.flmd.uscourts.gov/webforms/contact-cmecf>**

Verified 2026-07-28: the page reads "Complete the form below to request
assistance with CM/ECF. You must include both an email address and a telephone
number. A staff member will contact you as soon as possible."

So there is no Gmail draft for C5 — a draft addressed to a guessed
`clerk@flmd.uscourts.gov` would bounce or land nowhere. The letter text in
`c5-clerk-inquiry.md` is reflowed below to fit the form's fields.

## "How can we assist you?" — paste this

> I am an independent software developer in Tampa. I am not an attorney and I do
> not file in this district; I build an open-source tool that attorneys and their
> staff can run to help prepare filings, and I would like to build it to match
> what this court expects. I have a question I could not answer from the
> Administrative Procedures for Electronic Filing.
>
> The tool runs entirely on the filer's own computer. It assists with document
> validation, deadline checks, and pre-filling filing information, and it submits
> only through the filer's own CM/ECF login, in their own browser session, after
> the person at the keyboard reviews and authorizes that specific filing. No
> credentials are transmitted to or stored by any outside service, including mine.
>
> My question: does the court consider this kind of attorney-supervised,
> software-assisted preparation and submission to fall within the ordinary use of
> an attorney's ECF account by that attorney and their authorized staff? And is
> there anything the court would want filers using such tools to do, or to avoid,
> to stay squarely within its expectations?
>
> I would rather learn the court's preferences now than have filers discover them
> the hard way. If a different office or person is the right one for this
> question, I would appreciate a pointer.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Jackson Sanger
> Independent Developer, ECFiler Project
> realjacksons@gmail.com · github.com/jackson-jpeg/ecfiler

**Before submitting**, check whether the M.D. Fla. Administrative Procedures for
Electronic Filing contain an "authorized agent" or staff-use provision. If they
do, cite it by section number in the second paragraph — a question that shows
the filer read the local rules gets a materially better answer. If they don't,
send as written.

Estimated: 3 minutes, most of it the optional rules check.

S.D. Fla. is the optional second send, only after M.D. Fla. responds or four
weeks pass. Record the date in `contact-tracker.md`.
